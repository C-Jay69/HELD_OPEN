## 3. The one judgement call — read this bit

Moving the run to 16/17 February adds **twelve days** to the front of the block, and they had to go
somewhere. I have put them into **Chapter 17, which now reads "Days 53–70"**: the founding volumes
arrive on Day 53 and the chapter's reading, argument and consequences occupy the fortnight that
follows, with the Second Run being prepared across it.

It is the least intrusive option, because Chapter 17's only internal day-reference is a bulletin
("as of the thirtieth day, when the consolidation programme began"), which is unaffected. But if you
would rather the fortnight sat inside Chapter 18 — *"Days 59–82"* — or split between the two, say
so and it is a one-line change.

## 4. Two things I did **not** change, because they need your decision

1. **The Office's letter to the crew appears twice.** It is read in Chapter 22 — *"the letter from
   the office, the one with a typo in it… for the third time in a week"* — and then read again,
   for the first time, in Chapter 25. The typo in both is *Accommodated.* One of the two readings
   has to become a re-read or a reference. I did not touch either, because the fix changes the
   shape of a scene and that is yours.
2. **"Nine weeks to the day."** In Chapter 18, the vessel's withdrawal from the coastal service in
   April is described as nine weeks to the day from 17 February — which is 21 April, the day of the
   Elena interview. That now reads as a deliberate coincidence rather than an accident. If you want
   it to be exact, it is; if you want it loose, change it to "ten weeks".
