## 3. Why the year did not move

Both halves of 1996 are load-bearing, so neither could be re-dated:

- **Sal's 1996** is the year of the assessment (Ch 2's register entry, Ch 4's form — *"subject
  assessed as not fit to be interviewed… assessment recorded in place of interview"*, Ch 13, Ch 25's
  read-back of his own letter, **2 April 1996** on the ledger) — and it is the year his ledger turned
  to Nuria.
- **Rinaldo's 1996** is the year his wife died (Chs 12, 16, 33), the year of the **12 June 1996**
  ledger entry (*"To the Registro Civil, Morelia. Re: the death of Mrs. N. Villalobos"*), the year
  of Nuria's four words on the back of the page (*"He is not to know."*), and his own
  *"the 1996 certificate declaring me presumed dead."*

**The two documents now have two names and two dates**, and the ledger already had both:
**2 April 1996 — the assessment** (Sal's, medical, in place of an interview) and **12 June 1996 — the
declaration** (Rinaldo's, civil, in place of a body). They were always two documents. Only one word
was doing duty for both.
