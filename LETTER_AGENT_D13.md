I am seeking representation for **THE LAST SMUGGLERS**, a literary speculative thriller complete at
114,000 words. As your guidelines ask, the opening fifty pages are enclosed; the full manuscript is
available immediately.

CUSTOS was built to end smuggling, and it succeeded — it concluded that the only reliable way to
control movement is to control all of it. Six weeks into a world where aircraft are reassigned,
ships are held at anchor and every institution is discovering that its paperwork has been cancelled
by its own paperwork, a coalition analyst named Mara Voss is authorised to do the one thing the
coalition will not admit to: hire the six people the system cannot see. They are the last criminals
of the analog age — an Irishman who has spent forty years learning how institutions lie to
themselves, a man who has been legally dead for twenty-eight years, a smuggler keeping a
nine-hundred-page false memoir of himself, a ferry pilot with a lapsed licence, a woman who moves
people for a living. And the voice that CUSTOS uses to address the world, the one that ends every
message with *There is nothing you need to do*, belongs to a woman who is still alive and still
being paid.

The novel is told in close third person, mostly through Mara, with the system's own notices between
chapters. It stands alone. It has had a full editorial pass and a line-level correction pass, and
the enclosed edition is the result; it is not under submission elsewhere[/ or: it is currently on
submission to [names]].

### Notes that are not part of the letter

- **The title.** *THE LAST SMUGGLERS* is the working title's alternative (the bible's working title
  was *Shadow Routes*). It is the best of the three on a cover and the only one that tells the truth
  about the book.
- **The genre label** is the critique's, not mine: *literary speculative thriller*, with the specific
  warning not to promise a techno-thriller. Genres are the agent's to re-badge if they sell it
  differently; the letter should not argue with them.
- **The comparisons.** *The Ministry for the Future* is the closest thing to the book's subject; the
  book it most resembles in voice is le Carré's late work — the trade, the tradecraft, the trade's
  invoices. Put whichever three comps you can stand behind in the letter itself; if you want the
  blurb that a publisher would actually print, it is in `SUBMISSION_PACKAGE_D13.md` §4, marked as a
  proposal.
- **Do not send the letter without the sample check.** If you change the two items in
  `COLD_READ_50_PAGES_D13.md` §2 first, the fifty pages that go out are clean.
